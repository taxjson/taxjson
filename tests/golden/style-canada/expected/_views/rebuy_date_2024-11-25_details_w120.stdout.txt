HOW THE DATE IS SET
  - A loss is superficial when the same property is acquired within 30 days before or after the sale (settlement dates,
    s.54) by you or an affiliated person and still held 30 days after it.
  - REBUY ON is the first trading day whose buy settles after the latest loss sale's window closes (30 days after its
    settlement): the window's last day when a trade then settles the next day, the Friday before when it closes on a
    weekend (buy-check's safe-from date, and wash-radar's CLEARS for a COOLING or BLOCKED row; a VIOLATION's CLEARS is
    its last day to sell). When ticker.map or tobase.map books two listings as one, it is the later of their dates.
  - A long call on the shares is a replacement too (CA-PLAN-02); an option series is replaced only by the same series.

Scope: these verdicts cover this project's accounts only. A purchase by your spouse or common-law partner, or by a
corporation you or they control (affiliated persons, ITA s.251.1), within 30 days before or after a loss sale also makes
it superficial — those accounts are not in the project, so check them yourself.
