HOW THE DATE IS SET
  - A loss is a wash sale when the same security is bought within 30 days before or after the sale (trade dates, §1091)
    in any account, IRAs included; no still-held test.
  - REBUY ON is the first trading day from the 31st day after the latest loss sale a buy can still disallow; a loss
    already disallowed in full is not one.
  - After a loss on covering a short only a new short sale replaces it (§1091(e)); a long call is a note only
    (US-PLAN-02); futures, their options and broad-based index options (§1256) are outside §1091 (US-WASH-18).
