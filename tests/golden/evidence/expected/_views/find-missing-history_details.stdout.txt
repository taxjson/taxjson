A short sale you made and have not covered also shows here: no purchase is missing, and it is in no total until it is covered.
To fix a sale with no purchase in your files, in this order:
  1. Add an older export that holds the purchase to inputs/<account>/.
  2. Or enter the purchase as a .tt BUYSELL line with its real date and cost; `--write-purchases` drafts these from IB's Basis or a transfer's book value, to review.
  3. Only when it cannot be recovered: `tjs find-missing-history --write-missing-history` in the project writes .tt OPENING cost=unknown lines into inputs/<account>/missing_history.tt — review them, then `tjs run` (it reads them); those sales leave the totals and are reported by hand.
